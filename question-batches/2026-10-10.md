## Arithmetic (4)

### 5115. Find 18% of 750.

**Topic:** Percentages · **Difficulty:** Easy · **Exam:** LDA

A. 125
B. 130
C. 135
D. 145

**Answer:** C. 135

**Explanation:** Formula: (percentage ÷ 100) × total = (18/100) × 750 = 135. Shortcut: 10% is 75 and 8% is 60; total is 135.

**Source label:** Original exam-style practice; percentages

### 5116. A total of 144 is divided in the ratio 5:7. What is the larger share?

**Topic:** Ratio · **Difficulty:** Easy · **Exam:** LDA

A. 60
B. 72
C. 84
D. 96

**Answer:** C. 84

**Explanation:** Total parts = 5 + 7 = 12. One part = 144 ÷ 12 = 12. Larger share = 7 × 12 = 84. Check: 60 + 84 = 144.

**Source label:** Original exam-style practice; ratio

### 5117. Calculate simple interest on ₹15,000 at 8% per annum for 2 years.

**Topic:** Simple interest · **Difficulty:** Easy · **Exam:** LDA

A. ₹1,800
B. ₹2,000
C. ₹2,400
D. ₹2,800

**Answer:** C. ₹2,400

**Explanation:** SI = P×R×T/100 = 15,000×8×2/100 = ₹2,400. Total amount = ₹17,400.

**Source label:** Original exam-style practice; simple interest

### 5118. A finishes a job in 20 days and B in 30 days. How long do they take together at constant rates?

**Topic:** Time and work · **Difficulty:** Medium · **Exam:** SSC

A. 10 days
B. 12 days
C. 15 days
D. 25 days

**Answer:** B. 12 days

**Explanation:** A's rate is 1/20 job/day and B's is 1/30. Combined rate = 1/20 + 1/30 = 1/12 job/day, so the job takes 12 days.
