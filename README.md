# MPSC Smart Study Hub V5

A mobile-friendly GitHub Pages study hub for Meghalaya competitive examinations. Priority tracks: MPSC Inspector of Taxes, DSC East Khasi Hills LDA-cum-Typist and MPSC Secretariat LDA.

## Verified repository snapshot — 5 November 2026

- **821 active unique question records**: 592 embedded questions plus 12 loaded question batches.
- **111 records tagged PYQ**. A PYQ tag is not the same as a complete paper.
- **189 structured detailed explanations** across seven libraries: 49 History, 76 Polity, 20 English, 11 Arithmetic, 12 General Studies, 15 GST / Inspector of Taxes and 6 Current Affairs. This is **23.0% of the active bank**; 632 active IDs still need structured library explanations. The Arithmetic library adds worked solutions for 11 active questions (compound interest, percentages, ratio, simple interest, average, profit, time-and-work and speed); coverage is audited by ID in `audit/explanation-coverage-2026-11-05.json`.
- **Study Notes:** 14 subject libraries and 37 chapters.
- **MPSC Secretariat LDA May 2025 recruitment-cycle Series I:** 91 of 150 questions transcribed and checked against the official final answer key dated 17 December 2025; 59 remain.

## Evidence-led exam preparation

### MPSC Inspector of Taxes

An official MPSC question booklet for **Inspector of Tax, Series III** confirms that the examination for the April 2017 recruitment was held on **27 February 2021**. The complete booklet contains **150 questions for 300 marks in 2½ hours**, with no negative marking stated: Part A General English (50 questions / 100 marks), Part B General Knowledge & Aptitude (50 / 100) and Part C Mathematics (50 / 100). Each question carries 2 marks. The booklet is linked below.

Observed English format in this one paper: 10 error-identification questions, 10 idioms/phrases, 10 word-meaning/synonym questions, 10 antonyms and 10 fill-in-the-blanks. Part B is split by printed numbering into 25 General Knowledge questions (51–75) and 25 Aptitude questions (76–100). Mathematics contains 50 quantitative problems, including speed/distance, ages and averages, simple/compound interest, number properties and series, mensuration, percentages, ratios, profit/loss, partnership and work/rate problems. These are **within-paper observations**, not recurring multi-year frequencies.

The MPSC syllabus index reviewed on 4 November 2026 does not list a current post-specific Inspector of Taxes syllabus. No verified final answer key for the 2021 booklet was located in this review, so the hub does not attach unverified answer indices to it. The paper is one historical sample from the 2017 recruitment cycle, not a promise of the next pattern. Use the 50/50/50 structure as a historical timed-practice benchmark only; match future preparation to the next official advertisement and syllabus. GST, tax administration, constitutional finance and public audit remain useful role-related study, but the 2021 paper does not contain a separate technical GST section.

### DSC East Khasi Hills LDA-cum-Typist

Official district notices for the 2025–26 cycle record a written examination on 8 November 2025, a typing speed test on 9 December 2025 and a Khasi local-language paper on 17 January 2026. These notices confirm the stages, not a complete marks/section blueprint for a future cycle. Keep this track separate from the MPSC Secretariat LDA paper.

Preparation coverage: English, arithmetic, general awareness with Meghalaya focus, reasoning, computer basics, typing speed/accuracy and Khasi language where required by the next district notification.

### MPSC Secretariat LDA — May 2025 recruitment cycle

The Series I written paper was held on 13 September 2025. Its verified structure is 150 questions, 300 marks and 150 minutes: 75 General English plus 75 General Awareness & Aptitude. The hub holds all 75 English questions and the first 16 General Awareness questions (6 Science, 5 History, 5 Geography), for 91/150 total. Questions 92–150 remain to be transcribed and checked.

Editorial classification of the 75 English questions: 4 reading-comprehension, 6 idiom/phrase, 5 one-word substitution, 10 synonym/antonym, 5 direct/indirect speech, 10 grammar/sentence correction, 5 rearrangement, 5 cloze, 17 contextual vocabulary, 1 infinitive/purpose and 7 vocabulary/usage items. The official key does not rate difficulty; this is an editorial format count, not an official topic-weightage claim. The 16-question General Awareness sample is incomplete and must not be projected onto the remaining 59 questions.

## GKToday and specialist source strategy

GKToday (gktoday.in) is used as a recurring discovery and reference source across the full static General Studies range: Indian history, culture, geography, polity and Constitution, economy, science and technology, environment, international organisations, books/authors and related GK topics. Questions and explanations are written in original wording; protected question-bank wording and explanations are not copied.

High-stakes legal, constitutional, treaty, standards and time-sensitive facts are cross-checked against primary official sources. Meghalaya facts prioritise Meghalaya Government, MPSC and district-administration sources. English uses standard grammar/textbook references; arithmetic and reasoning use worked solutions; GST uses the CGST/IGST Acts, rules, CBIC and GST Portal guidance; computer and technical topics use specialist references.

## Quality rules

- Preserve official PYQ wording, option order, printed numbering and official answer-key mapping.
- Label original questions as practice, never as PYQs.
- Do not infer exam frequency from the size of question-bank categories.
- Do not enable exact-paper mode until the complete paper, options and official key are verified.
- Preserve the `mpscV4` local progress key, bookmarks, notes and study logs.
- Validate question IDs, four-option structure, answer indices, explanation coverage, loader paths, JSON, JavaScript and service-worker cache.
