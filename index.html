📰 Current Affairs — Meghalaya • India • World

Exam-focused briefing dated 9 October 2026. Check linked sources for updates before memorising fast-changing facts.

MEGHALAYA · 8 OCT 2026

Wangala Festival preparations reviewed

The Meghalaya Government press-release archive reports that Speaker Thomas Sangma reviewed preparations for the 50th Wangala Festival; the release notes a plan for 1,000 drums instead of 100 this year.

Exam link: Garo culture, festivals, state heritage and cultural tourism.

Source: Meghalaya Government press releases
Practice question

Wangala is traditionally associated with which community of Meghalaya?

Reveal answer and explanation

Answer: Garo community. Wangala is a major post-harvest festival of the Garo people, associated with thanksgiving and traditional music and dance.

INDIA · OCT 2026

National Household Travel Survey

A current-affairs report says the National Statistics Office (NSO), under the Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation, released key findings of India's first National Household Travel Survey, covering July 2025 to June 2026.

Exam link: NSO, MoSPI, national surveys and public policy. Verify exact figures from official releases before memorising.

Source: AffairsCloud daily digest (secondary source)
Practice question

The National Statistics Office functions under which Union ministry?

Reveal answer and explanation

Answer: Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI). The NSO conducts major official statistical activities and surveys.

WORLD · 8 OCT 2026

NATO's Steadfast Noon exercise

The Associated Press reports that NATO plans its annual Steadfast Noon exercise next week, hosted by Italy, involving aircraft from 17 NATO countries. NATO officials describe it as a routine exercise; no live nuclear warheads or ammunition are used.

Exam link: NATO, collective security, international relations and defence terminology.

Source: Associated Press, 8 October 2026
Practice question

NATO is primarily a:

Reveal answer and explanation

Answer: Collective-defence alliance. NATO is a political and military alliance founded by treaty in 1949; Article 5 concerns collective defence.

Show answer key and quick explanations

1-B Article 280. 2-B Article 279A. 3-C Full statehood: 21 January 1972. 4-B Khasi Hills, Jaintia Hills and Garo Hills ADCs. 5-B “Neither” is singular in this construction. 6-B Abrogate = repeal/cancel formally. 7-B The expression is “senior to”. 8-C 1916. 9-C 1946. 10-B Combined rate = 1/12 + 1/18 = 5/36 job/day; time = 36/5 = 7.2 days. 11-C SI = P×R×T/100 = ₹2,400. 12-C 240÷4 = 60 km/h. 13-C Platelets aid clotting. 14-B Scurvy. 15-B Central/eastern tropical Pacific warming. 16-B President. 17-C Article 51A. 18-B RBI lends to banks against eligible securities. 19-B GSTR-2B is an auto-drafted ITC statement. 20-C Reverse Charge Mechanism.
